About LocalLens

LocalLens is a free local guide for India. We want people to find good places in any city faster, and local businesses to be found without paying for ads.

Right now the site runs on sample listings so you can try every feature. Nothing here is a verified review, address or endorsement. As real businesses submit and we check them, sample cards will be replaced.

How it works

  • Choose a state or city from the location button.
  • Search or filter by category and budget.
  • Open a place for details and a map search link.
  • Save favourites and leave ratings. They stay in your browser.
